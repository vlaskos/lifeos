## Quick start

1. Press **+ Idea** on the dashboard and write any idea.
2. Pick its **Area** in Inbox and press **Processed** — it becomes a stream in the Parking column of that area.
3. Mark up to 3 streams as **Weekly focus**.

## How it works

- **Goal 🎯** — an outcome you want ("Run a half marathon").
- **Stream 🔄** — what you actually work on toward it ("Training plan, 3 runs a week"). A stream links to its goal through **Goal ↑**.
- **Areas** — big parts of your life. Yours:
{areas}
- **Status** — Parking (idea, not now) → Planned → In progress → Done.
- **Next step** — one verb, 30 minutes or less. If you can't name it, the stream isn't ready.
- **WIP limit** — at most {wip} streams In progress at once. Finish before you start.
- **Weekly focus** — up to 3 streams that get your time this week.

## What's on the dashboard

- **Weekly focus / Deadlines this month** — what needs you now.
- **Areas wheel** — how your goals spread across areas.
- **Inbox to process** — new ideas waiting for an area.
- **Overview tab** — all streams and goals by status, timeline, goal progress, and Stagnation (streams in progress that haven't changed for the longest time).
- **Area tabs** — one board per area.

## A simple rhythm

- **Daily:** capture ideas with + Idea; update the next step when you finish one.
- **Weekly (15 min):** process Inbox, check Stagnation, choose Weekly focus.

## Talk to it through Claude

With the LifeOS skills installed you can say: "save an idea: …", "let's process my inbox", "plan my week", "what's my status?", "close the stream X".

## Good to know

- Notion's free plan allows one chart per workspace; the Areas wheel uses it.
- Buttons can't check conditions — pick an Area before pressing Processed.
